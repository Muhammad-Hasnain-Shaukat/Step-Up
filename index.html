Centuries of Savoir-Faire

The Lahore & Karachi Atelier

Behind every Step Up silhouette lies more than 200 meticulous manual operations executed by generational Pakistani master cordwainers.

01

French Box Calfskin

Selected exclusively from the top 1% of raw hides in the Auvergne tanneries. Steeped in natural mimosa and chestnut extracts for velvety supple strength and immaculate breathability.

02

Hornbeam Wood Lasts

Every shoe is pulled over hand-sculpted aged hornbeam forms designed to cradle the natural 3D contours of the human tarsal arch, distributing body weight with aerodynamic poise.

03

Blake & Goodyear Welt

We reject bonded adhesives in favor of dual-thread locked chain stitching through the upper, insole, and outsole. Your Step Up shoes can be resoled indefinitely across lifetimes.

04

Natural Beeswax Patina

Layered over dozens of hours with natural alcohol pigments, walnut stain, and pure carnauba wax, yielding an iridescent depth of color that matures and deepens with age.

2
3

1. Hand-Blocked Calfskin Vamp

Zero synthetic lining. Full-grain French calf upper is blocked over heated forms for 48 hours to lock memory tension into the leather.

2. Lock-Stitched Blake Welt

Waxed linen thread connects the upper directly through the vegetable-tanned leather insole, providing supreme flexibility and water resistance.

3. Carbon-Fiber & Cork Core

A granulated oak cork bed molds uniquely to your footprint within 14 days, supported by a torsional carbon-fiber stabilizing shank.
